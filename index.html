Settings

—
The turn belongs to the slot, not the person. Slot 1 & 2 take nights, slot 3 & 4 take the weekend, then it moves down each cycle. Move an RN and they take that slot's turn.
Who is eligible for nights, in turn order. Each fortnight takes 2 from Group A and 2 from Group B (2 per night, 7 nights each), advancing down each group's turn and wrapping. Use ▲ / ▼ to change whose turn is next within their group; remove an RN to take them off nights entirely (they just work days). Weekends and day quotas are unaffected. If a group has fewer than 2 eligible, those nights are left short for you to fill by hand.
Minimum staff needed each weekday for each shift type. The generator fills weekdays up to these numbers; the health banner flags any day below them. Weekend day cover is the fixed 2-RN turn (edit D7 only). N7 is the night requirement and applies every day — it is staffed by the Night cycle turn (that many RNs from Group A, and that many from Group B, alternating weeks), never by day shifts.
Extra shift types you place by hand (like E10, S11). They appear in the cell picker, legend and exports, and count as working shifts — but the automatic weekday fill only ever uses the core day shifts.
Support-staff groups (not part of the RN rules). Rename a group, and add or remove people in it.
